# ALComplete

**A**uto **L**evels **Complete**. Open a level, tap **Solve & Play**, and watch it finish itself.

There is no macro to record and no botting first. ALComplete searches the level for a
path that never touches a hazard, picks the simplest one, and plays it back tick-exact.

- Draggable floating circle with a shiny, animated menu (also in the pause menu)
- Categorised menu: Solver, Route, Visuals, Appearance, Safety, Diagnostics, About
- Opacity, scale, accent colour and a changeable button icon
- Works on the real game, so orbs, pads, portals and every gamemode are handled by GD itself
- **Safe Mode** (on by default): completions are not saved or submitted

Everything is configured from inside the menu. No subscription, no account.
